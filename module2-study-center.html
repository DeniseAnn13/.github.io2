Rapid summary

Sensation

  • Receptors transduce physical or chemical energy into neural activity.
  • Thalamic relays commonly send sensory information to primary cortical areas.
  • Perception combines receptor signals, cortical organization, and experience.

Movement

  • Cortex plans and executes voluntary movement.
  • Cerebellum improves accuracy, timing, and learning.
  • Basal ganglia help select and initiate actions.

Regulation

  • Homeostasis maintains targets; allostasis anticipates demands.
  • Temperature, thirst, and appetite use sensors, hormones, and behavior.
  • Obesity involves genes, psychology, environment, and adaptation.

Sleep

  • Light is a major circadian zeitgeber.
  • Sleep drugs act on GABA, melatonin, orexin, histamine, or adenosine.
  • Sleep theories emphasize restoration and adaptation.
