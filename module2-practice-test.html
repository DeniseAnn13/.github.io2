Module 2 Practice Test

Complete the blanks and select True or False.

A. Fill in the blank

1. The ________ stream helps identify objects and faces.
2. The ossicles transmit sound from the eardrum to the ________.
3. Sharp, relatively fast pain is carried largely by ________ fibers.
4. Salt and sour taste involve ion-based transduction; sweet and umami use ________ receptors.
5. The ________ helps coordinate movement timing and accuracy.
6. Parkinson’s disease is strongly associated with loss of ________ neurons.
7. Maintaining stability by anticipating changing demands is called ________.
8. Osmotic thirst is triggered by increased concentration of body ________.
9. Light is a major ________, or environmental time cue.
10. Caffeine promotes alertness partly by blocking ________ receptors.

B. True or False

1. Nasal retinal fibers cross at the optic chiasm.
2. Rods provide the sharpest color vision.
3. The dorsal column pathway carries pain and temperature.
4. Huntington’s disease commonly produces involuntary choreiform movements.
5. Hypovolemic thirst occurs after major loss of blood volume or fluids.
6. Leptin is a major signal that usually reflects stored body energy.
7. Benzodiazepines reduce neural activity partly through GABA-A receptors.
8. Orexin normally promotes wakefulness.
Answer key

Fill-ins: ventral; oval window; A-delta; T1R; cerebellum; dopamine; allostasis; fluids; zeitgeber; adenosine.

True/false: 1 T, 2 F, 3 F, 4 T, 5 T, 6 T, 7 T, 8 T.
