Module 2 Mock Exam

Attempt all questions before opening the answer key. Suggested time: 35–45 minutes.

1. Trace visual information from the retina to the primary visual cortex.

2. Explain trichromatic theory and its relationship to the three cone types.

3. Describe cochlear transduction.

4. Compare A-delta and C fibers.

5. Which receptor mediates smell, and how is odor coding accomplished?

6. What are the roles of the premotor cortex and supplementary motor area?

7. Compare Parkinson’s disease and Huntington’s disease.

8. Compare homeostasis and allostasis with examples.

9. Describe osmotic and hypovolemic thirst.

10. Why can weight loss become difficult after obesity?

11. True or False: The ventral visual stream primarily supports spatial location and visually guided action.

12. Which receptor system is enhanced by benzodiazepines?

Open answer key

1. Retina → optic nerves → optic chiasm → optic tracts → LGN → optic radiations → primary visual cortex. Nasal fibers cross; each hemisphere receives the opposite visual field.

2. S, M, and L cones respond most to short, medium, and long wavelengths. Color is inferred from relative cone activity.

3. Ossicles move the oval window; fluid and basilar-membrane motion bend hair-cell stereocilia, producing receptor potentials and transmitter release onto auditory nerve fibers.

4. A-delta fibers are myelinated and transmit sharp, well-localized first pain; C fibers are unmyelinated and transmit slower, aching pain.

5. G-protein-coupled olfactory receptors on sensory neurons; odors produce receptor combinations and spatial/temporal patterns.

6. Premotor cortex uses external cues to prepare movement; supplementary motor area sequences and organizes internally initiated actions.

7. Parkinson’s involves dopamine-neuron loss and reduced initiation; Huntington’s involves striatal degeneration and involuntary chorea.

8. Homeostasis defends set points. Allostasis changes responses to anticipated or changing demands.

9. Osmotic thirst follows concentrated body fluids. Hypovolemic thirst follows reduced blood or fluid volume.

10. After weight loss, hunger signaling may rise, energy expenditure may fall, and leptin and allostatic adaptations defend higher body-mass set points.

11. False. The dorsal stream is mainly spatial/location and action; the ventral stream identifies objects.

12. GABA-A.
